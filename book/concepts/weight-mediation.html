Weight Mediation of CR Effects

Key quantitative result: only 48.5% (95% CI 22.6 to 82.2%) of the effect of CR on the composite biomarker index was mediated by weight loss — i.e., about half of CR's effect operates through weight-independent pathways.

Model 3 (both CR and weight change as predictors)

CR effect on index−2.2 → −1.2 (95% CI −2.0 to −0.3)
Weight-loss effect on index0.22 → 0.16 (95% CI 0.09 to 0.23)

Reading: the composite index has a statistically significant residual direct effect (−1.34) — CR improves the index through both weight-dependent and weight-independent mechanisms. Significant weight-mediated (indirect) effects were found for CRP, IL-6, and insulin; CysC, GDF-15, TNF-R1 indirect effects were not significant. MEDIFAST's mediation model did not converge (small n=31).
