Limitations & Future Directions

Limitations

  1. Single research network: all 7 trials came from the Wake Forest IASDR — generalizability to broader populations is limited.
  2. Short durations: intervention durations ranged from 6 to 18 months (IM-FIT 5 mo, MEDIFAST 6 mo, INFINITE 20 wk, HALLO-P 9 mo, CLIP/EMPOWER/IDEA 18 mo); findings are best read as evidence that CR favorably modified inflammatory and metabolic biomarkers, not yet as proof of long-term disease/mortality benefit.
  3. Heterogeneity: GDF-15 and TNF-R1 study-specific estimates were variable; MEDIFAST (n=31) was underpowered (mediation model did not converge).
  4. Exercise confounding: exercise modality could not be fully adjusted for (confounded with study); sensitivity analysis restricted to CLIP/EMPOWER/IDEA (the only trials with exercise variation) left primary findings unchanged.
  5. Composite vs. other aging measures: correlation between this index and epigenetic clocks (DunedinPACE, static epigenetic age) or frailty indices was not addressed.
  6. Inferential mechanisms: AMPK/SIRT1/mTOR/autophagy pathways are proposed, not directly measured in this analysis.

Future directions

  • Validate the composite index in independent, more diverse populations.
  • Establish its relationship to hard endpoints: mortality, disability, functional decline.
  • Correlate the index with epigenetic clocks and validated frailty indices to position it within the geroscience biomarker landscape.
  • Apply the index to large epidemiologic cohorts and pragmatic trials as a surrogate endpoint for aging interventions.
  • Longer follow-up to test whether index improvement predicts slower pace of aging.
