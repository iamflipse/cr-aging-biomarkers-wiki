Pooled Trial Landscape

The analysis pooled 7 randomized CR trials (n = 829; 494 CR / 335 no-CR) from the Wake Forest Integrated Aging Studies Databank and Repository (IASDR).

TrialYearsN (pooled)DurationCR target / delivery
CLIP2006–200918218 mo7–10% weight loss, dietary counseling; adults 60–79 y, BMI ≥28 with CVD/cardiometabolic dysfunction + mobility limitation
EMPOWER2016–20196118 mo7–10% WL, dietary counseling + aerobic exercise or social-cognitive behavioral program; ages 65–86, BMI 30–45
HALLO-P2022–2024909 mo3 arms: CR, remote CR, or time-restricted eating (see IF wiki); ages ≥60, BMI >27
IDEA2006–201024418 mo~10–15% WL, structured dietary counseling; ≥55 y, BMI 27–41, knee OA
IM-FIT2010–2013605 mo8–10% WL + moderate-intensity resistance training
INFINITE2009–201412120 wk5–11% WL via dietary counseling + partial meal replacement; aerobic ± CR
MEDIFAST2016–2019316 mo7–10% WL, high-protein program with partial meal replacement

Population: mean age 67.5 y, 67.4% female, 82% white; baseline BMI 30.4–35.6 kg/m²; mean weight 85.3–97.9 kg.

Analytic setup: 100 imputed datasets (MICE, 14 variables), plate = random batch effect in mixed models, study = fixed effect; CR treated as binary exposure, weight change as downstream consequence.
