# Weight Mediation of CR Effects

**Key quantitative result:** only **48.5% (95% CI 22.6 to 82.2%)** of the effect of CR on the composite biomarker index was mediated by weight loss — i.e., **about half of CR's effect operates through weight-independent pathways**.

## Model 3 (both CR and weight change as predictors)

| Effect | Unadjusted → adjusted |
|---|---|
| CR effect on index | −2.2 → **−1.2** (95% CI −2.0 to −0.3) |
| Weight-loss effect on index | 0.22 → **0.16** (95% CI 0.09 to 0.23) |

## Mediation analysis (Table 3, bootstrap CIs, 1000 samples)

| Outcome | Total effect | Direct (weight-independent) | Indirect (via weight) | % mediated |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| log CRP | −0.36 (−0.50, −0.22) | −0.14 (−0.30, 0.02) | −0.22 (−0.31, −0.14) | 64.9 (34.7, 107.5) |
| log IL-6 | −0.13 (−0.22, −0.04) | −0.06 (−0.17, 0.05) | −0.06 (−0.12, −0.01) | 58.7 (2.6, 143.9) |
| Cystatin C | 0.03 (−0.01, 0.08) | 0.06 (−0.002, 0.12) | −0.02 (−0.05, 0.003) | −54.3 (−693.3, 601.6) |
| Insulin | −14.62 (−21.90, −7.34) | −3.34 (−11.84, 5.16) | −12.19 (−16.26, −8.33) | 85.1 (48.5, 156.8) |
| GDF-15 | −21.53 (−60.37, 17.30) | −20.96 (−67.92, 25.99) | 2.94 (−22.50, 27.54) | −6.3 (−362.3, 405.0) |
| TNF-R1 | −45.75 (−97.88, 6.39) | −33.51 (−95.94, 28.93) | −12.17 (−38.82, 15.98) | 16.0 (−88.2, 267.3) |
| **Quintile sum score** | **−1.99 (−2.72, −1.26)** | **−1.34 (−2.24, −0.44)** | **−0.94 (−1.40, −0.49)** | **48.5 (22.6, 82.2)** |

**Reading:** the composite index has a statistically significant **residual direct effect** (−1.34) — CR improves the index through **both** weight-dependent and weight-independent mechanisms. Significant weight-mediated (indirect) effects were found for **CRP, IL-6, and insulin**; CysC, GDF-15, TNF-R1 indirect effects were not significant. MEDIFAST's mediation model did not converge (small n=31).
