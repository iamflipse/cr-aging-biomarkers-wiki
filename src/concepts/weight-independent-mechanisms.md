# Weight-Independent Mechanisms

The persistent CR effect after adjusting for weight change implies **weight-independent biological pathways**. The paper discusses several candidate mechanisms (inferential in this analysis, not directly measured):

- **AMPK activation + SIRT1 upregulation + mTOR suppression:** nutrient-sensing pathways; mTOR suppression stimulates **autophagy** of damaged proteins and organelles.
- **Oxidative stress + chronic low-grade inflammation:** the same AMPK/SIRT1/mTOR mechanisms are linked to reducing oxidative stress and attenuating chronic inflammation — a plausible bridge to the observed CRP and IL-6 improvements independent of weight loss.
- **Mitochondrial adaptations & immunometabolic shifts** in innate immune cell function — implicated in CR benefits in animal models; possible pathway to the insulin-sensitivity improvements observed.

**Animal-model support:**
- Lifespan extension with ~40% CR is **independent of body fat**: in some studies the longest-lived CR animals had the **highest fat mass**; in outbred mice, those maintaining greater weight/fat under CR showed greatest longevity.
- Consistent with CALERIE/SELECT evidence of **metabolic and inflammatory benefits beyond weight loss** (SELECT: ~30% MACE reduction driven largely by waist-circumference change, not total weight loss).
