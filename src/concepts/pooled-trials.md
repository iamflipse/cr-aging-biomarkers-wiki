# Pooled Trial Landscape

The analysis pooled **7 randomized CR trials** (n = 829; 494 CR / 335 no-CR) from the **Wake Forest Integrated Aging Studies Databank and Repository (IASDR)**.

| Trial | Years | N (pooled) | Duration | CR target / delivery |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CLIP | 2006–2009 | 182 | 18 mo | 7–10% weight loss, dietary counseling; adults 60–79 y, BMI ≥28 with CVD/cardiometabolic dysfunction + mobility limitation |
| EMPOWER | 2016–2019 | 61 | 18 mo | 7–10% WL, dietary counseling + aerobic exercise or social-cognitive behavioral program; ages 65–86, BMI 30–45 |
| HALLO-P | 2022–2024 | 90 | 9 mo | 3 arms: CR, remote CR, or **time-restricted eating** (see [IF wiki](https://iamflipse.github.io/if-chrononutrition-wiki/)); ages ≥60, BMI >27 |
| IDEA | 2006–2010 | 244 | 18 mo | ~10–15% WL, structured dietary counseling; ≥55 y, BMI 27–41, knee OA |
| IM-FIT | 2010–2013 | 60 | 5 mo | 8–10% WL + moderate-intensity resistance training |
| INFINITE | 2009–2014 | 121 | 20 wk | 5–11% WL via dietary counseling + partial meal replacement; aerobic ± CR |
| MEDIFAST | 2016–2019 | 31 | 6 mo | 7–10% WL, high-protein program with partial meal replacement |

**Population:** mean age 67.5 y, 67.4% female, 82% white; baseline BMI 30.4–35.6 kg/m²; mean weight 85.3–97.9 kg.

**Analytic setup:** 100 imputed datasets (MICE, 14 variables), plate = random batch effect in mixed models, study = fixed effect; CR treated as binary exposure, weight change as downstream consequence.
