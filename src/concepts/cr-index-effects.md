# CR Effects on the Index

## Composite index (quintile sum score)

- **CR → index:** **−2.2** (95% CI −2.9 to −1.5) — CR was consistently associated with index improvement; study-specific estimates were all < 0 (Supp. Fig 1a).
- **After adjusting for weight change (model 3):** the CR effect attenuated to **−1.2** (95% CI −2.0 to −0.3) — the residual **direct (weight-independent) effect** (Supp. Fig 1c).
- Imputed and non-imputed analyses gave consistent conclusions.

## Individual biomarkers (CR vs no-CR, overall mean group differences)

| Biomarker | Group difference (95% CI) | Significant? |
|---|---|---|
| CRP (log) | 1.09 mg/L (0.28, 1.89) | ✅ |
| Insulin | 13.34 pmol/mL (6.17, 20.51) | ✅ |
| IL-6 | −0.07 pg/mL (−1.09, 0.94) | ❌ |
| Cystatin C | −0.03 mg/L (−0.08, 0.02) | ❌ |
| GDF-15 | 15.95 pg/mL (−21.89, 53.79) | ❌ (heterogeneous) |
| TNF-R1 | 37.02 pg/mL (−12.42, 86.47) | ❌ (heterogeneous) |

**Interpretation:** the most robust CR-associated changes were in **inflammatory and metabolic markers (CRP, insulin)**; GDF-15 and TNF-R1 showed considerable between-study heterogeneity (MEDIFAST was the exception where the direction differed).
